Ten cursor morphs for the section navigation.

Each stage replicates the live left rail from /case-study/index.html — same 196px column, 14px Beatrice, #aaa idle, black on hover and active. Only the cursor behaviour changes.

Move the pointer inside a dashed stage; the native cursor is hidden there. Press to see the pressed state. Keyboard users keep the standard focus ring — every morph is decorative only.
